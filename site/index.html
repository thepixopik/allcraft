03 / Principles

Shared by every craft

Built the hard way.

Every Crafting App is written from scratch in Rust and held to the same rules.

  1. 01

    Open source

    Every line is on GitHub under permissive MIT / Apache-2.0 licenses. Read it, fork it, build on it.

  2. 02

    Native, not wrapped

    Pure Rust compiled to WebGPU + WebAssembly and native desktop binaries. Zero Electron bloat.

  3. 03

    Familiar from day one

    Layouts, tools and shortcuts that working professionals already know, so there is nothing to relearn.

  4. 04

    Your files, your machine

    Everything runs 100% locally on your own device. Zero cloud uploads or servers between you and your work.

  5. 05

    Agent-ready

    Drive every app from a CLI, a JSON control channel or an MCP server, built for automation and AI agents.

  6. 06

    Instant in your browser

    Decompressed WebAssembly binaries lock into browser CacheStorage so repeat launches open in under 0.25 seconds.

04 / Frequently Asked Questions

Licensing, Privacy & Alternatives

Is AllCraft a free alternative to Adobe Creative Cloud, Microsoft Office, and AutoCAD?

Yes. AllCraft (allcraft.app) unites 15 free, open-source (MIT / Apache-2.0) pure-Rust applications in your browser covering Photoshop (PhotoCraft), Illustrator (VectorCraft), Premiere Pro (FilmCraft), Lightroom (LightCraft), Acrobat Pro (PdfCraft), After Effects (EffectCraft), Animate (AniCraft), InDesign (DesignCraft), Substance 3D (TextureCraft & MaterialCraft), Audition (SoundCraft), Word (WordCraft), PowerPoint (DeckCraft), AutoCAD (CADCraft), and Excel (GridCraft).

Are my files uploaded to any cloud server on allcraft.app?

Never. Every AllCraft editor compiles to WebAssembly and runs 100% locally in your browser RAM and GPU. Your PSD, RAW, PDF, DWG, video, and XLSX files never leave your device.

How is AllCraft related to the open-source Craft / ArtCraft ecosystem?

AllCraft Studio is an independent MIT/Apache-2.0 open-source web cloud build of the 15 pure-Rust Craft repositories (not affiliated with the upstream ArtCraft Team), adding instant CacheStorage Wasm caching and 1-click browser hosting across all 15 tools. Read the full Craft / ArtCraft Web Guide.
